# visionarygamingstudio.com
#
# Two different appetites, handled separately.
#
# SEARCH ENGINES are welcome and wanted — the whole point of the site is to be
# found. Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo and friends fall under the catch-all below.
#
# AI TRAINING CRAWLERS, CONTENT SCRAPERS AND SEO DATA BROKERS are not. The
# artwork, the circuits, the screenshots and the copy are not training material
# and not a dataset, and none of it is licensed for reuse. Every agent named
# below is refused the whole site.
#
# NOTE, honestly: robots.txt is a request, not a lock. It is obeyed by the
# large, reputable crawlers and ignored by anyone who came here to take
# something. It is worth having for exactly that reason and no more.

# -- link-preview crawlers: ALWAYS allowed -------------------------------
# These are what render a share card on Facebook, X, LinkedIn, Slack,
# Discord, WhatsApp, Telegram and Pinterest. They are NOT scrapers and
# blocking one silently turns every shared link into a bare grey box.
# Listed explicitly, and first, because a robots.txt match is by most
# specific user-agent group: naming them here means no future broad
# Disallow can ever swallow them by accident.
#

# while "FacebookBot" and "Meta-ExternalAgent" in the refused block are
