"As a solo developer, my mission is to deliver pure kinetic velocity and unforgettable arcade thrills without publisher bloat. Every line of code, every voxel model, and every physics calculation is built with relentless dedication to gameplay feel."

Nelson Rios Perez is an independent game architect, systems programmer, and technical artist creating high-octane indie titles with bespoke voxel physics technology.

Operating as a true solo developer under Visionary Gaming Studio, Nelson designs and engineers every layer of production: custom physics engines, GLSL rendering shaders, Blender 3D voxel models, procedural audio synthesis, UI/UX, and multi-platform publishing.
