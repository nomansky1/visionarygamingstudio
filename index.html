VoxKart: Combat Circuit

Combat car racing you can play offline. Race 55 voxel cars on 15 hand-built circuits, and shoot your way to the front.

  • 55 cars
  • 15 circuits
  • 5 race modes
  • 11 languages
Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play

Free, with optional one-time purchases. iPhone and iPad (iOS 15 or later) and Android 8.0 or later. voxkartracing.com

  • Build your own car

    Model a car voxel by voxel in the Car Builder, bolt on a weapon, paint it, then race it.

  • Guns on the grid

    Gatlings, rocket pods, homing missiles, mines, a laser pulse and a ram bar. Shots lock on inside a 20° cone.

  • Drift for boost

    Hold a drift through a long corner to bank a charge. Let go, and it fires as boost.

  • Plays offline

    No account, no sign-in, no server. Everything runs on your phone, even in aeroplane mode.

The studio

Built and published in-house

Visionary Gaming Studio is an independent studio founded by Nelson Rios Perez. We design, program and publish our own games, from the physics to the store listing, and we build them for phones first.

Nelson Rios PerezFounder

  • Offline by design

    No accounts, no sign-in, no servers. Our games keep working on a plane.

  • Fair about money

    Everything in VoxKart can be won by playing. Its purchases are one-time: no subscriptions, no loot boxes, no energy timers.

  • Made for two thumbs

    Landscape layouts, controls built for a touch screen, and a 60 fps target on mid-range phones.
